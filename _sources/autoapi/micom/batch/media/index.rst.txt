.. py:function:: process_medium(medium, samples)

   Prepare a medium for simulation.

   This will set the index to the reaction ID and convert it to a sample-wise medium if it is not already.

   :param medium: The medium to process. Should have columns `reaction`, `flux`, and optionally `sample_id`.
   :type medium: pd.DataFrame
   :param samples: The sample IDs to include in the medium.
   :type samples: list of str

   :returns: The processed medium with columns `reaction`, `flux`, and `sample_id`.
   :rtype: pd.DataFrame
